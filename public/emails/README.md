### Suggested subject / preview

- **Subject:** A few resources for this Sunday
- **Preview text:** Optional resources + class slides with primary sources — three themes, not a script.

### Design notes

- Scripture/talk buttons in the **email** use Church universal links (`churchofjesuschrist.org/study…`) so they open in **Gospel Library** on iOS/Android when the app is installed.
- The slideshow is **class-facing** — sparse scripture, place-yourself, discuss prompts. Not teacher-prep notes.
- “Go deeper” means **primary sources** on the screen: Cyrus Cylinder, Josephus, Sanballat’s open letter, first-person memoir texts — the same move as dropping a deposition into a Doctrine and Covenants lesson.

### Serving every class (ages 11 through adult)

One email goes to all Sunday School teachers, so the scripture block is shared and the *delivery* is what changes:

- Each theme has an **“Adapt for your class”** toggle with ideas for ages 11–14, 15–18, and adults — what to lead with, what to ask, what to cut.
- The deck ships **one file with two tracks**. Tapping **Youth** in the top corner (or adding `?track=youth`) drops the densest slides (Josephus, the side-by-side, the Whitney and Joseph Fielding Smith quotes) and swaps in shorter questions plus a build-a-tower activity. Adult track is 36 slides; youth is 33.
- Track choice persists in `localStorage`, so a teacher sets it once.
