### Design notes

- Scripture/talk buttons in the **email** use Church universal links (`churchofjesuschrist.org/study…`) so they open in **Gospel Library** on iOS/Android when the app is installed.
- The slideshow is **class-facing** — sparse scripture, place-yourself, discuss prompts. Not teacher-prep notes.
- “Go deeper” means **primary sources** on the screen: Cyrus Cylinder, Josephus, Sanballat’s open letter, first-person memoir texts — the same move as dropping a deposition into a Doctrine and Covenants lesson.
