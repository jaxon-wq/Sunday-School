Job: the man who lost almost everything — and still chose God

Job 1–3; 19; 38–40; 42 · “Yet Will I Trust in Him”

Job’s story in three moments. Everything else this week is commentary.

Moment 1 · Job 2:13

His friends just sat with him

“They sat down with him upon the ground seven days and seven nights, and none spake a word unto him.”

Seven days. No advice, no speeches. When someone is sad, being there comes before saying anything.

Moment 2 · Job 19:25

Job still trusted God

“I know that my redeemer liveth.”

A redeemer was the family member who came to buy you back when you lost everything. Job is saying: my family is coming for me. He said it before anything got better.

Moment 3 · Job 38:4, 7

God answered from the whirlwind

“Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? … When the morning stars sang together?”

God didn’t explain why. He showed Job the sea and the stars. Job never got an answer to “why.” He got God, and that was enough (Job 42:5).

Think about it

What do you know about Jesus that a hard day can’t take away?

This week: sit with someone who needs it. No fixing, no advice. Just stay.
